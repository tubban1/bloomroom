# Bloomroom arrangement models

All 25 downloaded botanical GLBs are prepared as a single cuttable unit: one
flower head, one flower stem or flowering spike, or one foliage leaf or sprig.
Each unit uses Y-up coordinates and a stem attachment at `(0, 0, 0)`. On a
complete stem this is the cut end; on a head it is the calyx or receptacle.
Lily petals naturally extend a little below that attachment. The model
manifest in `units.json` records its vertical bounds; one studio unit is
28 cm. A user's stem-height setting measures from the cut end to the highest
petal or leaf, including the flower head. Runtime height calibration keeps
70 cm comparable across species. Flower heads
are calibrated by front-facing width; complete stems are calibrated to a
representative horizontal span at 70 cm. The targets in
`prepare-arrangement-assets.py` are cut-flower presentation sizes, not claims
that every cultivar in a species has the same dimensions. Some downloaded
models had disproportionate branch spread, so their horizontal span is
corrected during preparation while their cut-to-tip height stays fixed.

The source downloads are not served. To rebuild, run
`prepare-arrangement-assets.py SOURCE_DIR OUTPUT_DIR` with the bundled Python
runtime and then `node scripts/simplify-arrangement-assets.mjs OUTPUT_DIR`.
After copying the GLBs and `units.json` here, run
`refine-flower-heads.py UNREFINED_COPY public/models` to remove stray foliage
from hydrangea, tulip, lily, chamomile and daisy and place their stem
attachments. Run `refine-flower-stems.py UNREFINED_COPY public/models` to
remove side foliage from carnation, spray chrysanthemum and snowdrop. Run
`render-model-audit.py` and review `docs/model-audit.jpg` before publishing.
The scripts explicitly remove source pots, vases, soil,
scan markers, extra plants and lower-detail copies where they exist. Model
textures are resized to at most 1024 px and unused mesh vertices are removed.

Some supplied models are flower heads without a usable cut stem. For rose,
tulip, daisy, poppy, peony, hydrangea, sunflower, orchid, lily, anemone,
chamomile and gerbera, the studio renders a simple cut stem below the
original botanical head or leaf. Lily-of-the-valley was supplied as detached
bells; its original bells are joined by a cut stem and pedicels. No bloom or
leaf surface is generated by the studio.
Ivy retains one original branching shoot with its leaves and cut end.

See `credits.json` and `credits.html` for each author, source, license and
modification. Original downloads, credentials and signed URLs are not shipped.

## Verified additions (2026-10-03)

Seven additional cut units: ranunculus, narcissus, dahlia, amaryllis, astrantia, eryngium and pine cone. All are registered in `units.json` and `credits.json`. The originally downloaded sword, shipwreck, point cloud and unsuitable/restricted variants are not served. See `docs/swiss-florals-integration.md` for the audit and calibrations.

Raw originals and verified replacement downloads are preserved locally under `.asset-sources/`, excluded from Git and deployment. With NumPy/Pillow and Blender installed, reproduce the extraction using:

```sh
python3 scripts/prepare-swiss-florals.py .asset-sources/swiss-originals .asset-sources/swiss-replacements public/models
```
# Carnation refinement

The carnation now uses the original downloaded petals and calyx as a rigid head,
with an 8 cm presentation diameter and a restrained curved studio stem. Changing
stem height no longer stretches the source stalk or bloom. Its model URL uses a
species-specific cache version so existing clients receive the new geometry.
Reproduce from the normalized full-stem backup with `scripts/refine-carnation.py`.
